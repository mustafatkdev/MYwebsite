About Roj Quest

Roj Quest is a programming quiz game built for a university festival. It focuses on the C language and runs fully in the browser, with no server or account.

Scoring

Easy questions are worth 10 points, medium 20, and hard 30. A correct answer also adds a time bonus of up to 10 points. XP equals the points you score, and every 100 XP is one level.

Modes

Topic rounds use the 4 questions of one category with 20 seconds each. Mixed Quest is 10 random questions with 20 seconds each. Speed Round is 10 random questions with only 10 seconds each.

Your data

No registration is needed. Your name and XP are saved in this browser. When you finish a quiz, your name and score are also sent to the shared leaderboard if the organiser has turned it on. The button below erases only the data saved in this browser.
